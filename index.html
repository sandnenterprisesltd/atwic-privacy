Privacy Policy

Around the World in Clips · Last updated 13 November 2026

Around the World in Clips is a travel video page on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, run by S&N Enterprises Limited in New Zealand. This policy covers the small tool we use to publish our own videos and photos to our own pages, and to reply to comments on them.

What we collect

Our tool does not ask anyone to sign in and does not collect personal information from visitors. When we reply to comments on our own posts, the tool reads the comment text and the commenter's public name as shown by Facebook or Instagram, only so we can reply.

We also read our own page statistics (views, likes, followers) to see how our posts are doing.

How we use it

  • To publish our own content to our own pages.
  • To reply to comments on our posts.
  • To review how our own posts perform.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

Comment details are only kept for as long as needed to reply. Page statistics are kept as overall totals only.

Deleting your data

If you'd like any information about you removed, email us at sandnenterprisesltd@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also delete your own comments on Facebook or Instagram at any time.
