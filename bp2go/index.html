Privacy Policy

BP2Go Putaruru, BP2Go Maungatapu and BP2Go Te Puke · Last updated 13 November 2026

The BP2Go Putaruru, Maungatapu and Te Puke service stations are run by BK Traders Limited in New Zealand. This policy covers the small tool we use to post our in-store specials and fuel offers to our own Facebook pages. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

Our posting tool does not ask anyone to sign in and does not collect personal information from customers or visitors. It only publishes our own posts to our own pages. If it reads comments on our posts, it sees only the comment text and the commenter's public name as shown by Facebook, so we can reply.

We also read our own page statistics (reach, likes, followers) to see how our posts are doing.

How we use it

  • To post our specials and fuel offers to our own pages.
  • To reply to comments on our posts.
  • To review how our own posts perform.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

Comment details are only kept for as long as needed to reply. Page statistics are kept as overall totals only.

Deleting your data

If you'd like any information about you removed, email us at sandeep@bktradersltd.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also delete your own comments on Facebook at any time.
